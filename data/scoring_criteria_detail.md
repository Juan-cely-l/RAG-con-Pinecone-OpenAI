# Detailed MMA Scoring Criteria (Educational Summary)

This file is an unofficial educational summary of how judges score rounds under the Unified Rules of MMA. It complements mma_rules_and_judging.md and is not an official rulebook.

## Order of the scoring criteria

Judges apply the criteria in a strict order:

1. Effective striking and effective grappling (the primary criterion). This should be the deciding factor in the large majority of rounds.
2. Effective aggressiveness. It is only considered when effective striking and grappling are even.
3. Fighting area control. It is only considered when all previous criteria are even.

## Effective striking

Effective striking means legal strikes that have an immediate or cumulative impact with the potential to contribute to the end of the fight. Impact is valued over the raw number of strikes landed.

## Effective grappling

Effective grappling means successful takedowns, reversals, and submission attempts, as well as advancing to a dominant position. A takedown or position that produces no meaningful offense counts for less than one that creates real danger for the opponent.

## Effective aggressiveness

Effective aggressiveness means aggressively making an attempt to finish the fight. Simply moving forward or chasing an opponent without landing effective offense is not effective aggressiveness.

## Fighting area control

Fighting area control means dictating the pace, place, and position of the bout.

## The 10-9 round

A 10-9 round is a round where one fighter wins by a close margin. It is the most common score.

## The 10-8 round

A 10-8 round is a round where one fighter wins by a large margin. Judges consider three factors:

- Impact: the fighter's offense clearly diminishes the opponent's ability (for example, knockdowns or visible damage).
- Dominance: the opponent is forced to defend almost the entire round without meaningful offense.
- Duration: the amount of time a fighter spends imposing impact or dominance.

Judges should consider giving a 10-8 when a fighter shows dominance and duration, or impact that is clearly significant. A 10-8 is not limited to rounds with a near-finish.

## The 10-7 round

A 10-7 round is when one fighter totally overwhelms the opponent in striking or grappling. It is rare.

## The 10-10 round

A 10-10 round is possible when the two fighters are completely even, but under the modern criteria judges are asked to give it only in very rare cases, because there is almost always some difference between the fighters.

## Point deductions and the final score

When the referee deducts a point for a foul, the judges subtract it from the offending fighter's score in that round. For example, a round scored 10-9 for a fighter who was penalized one point becomes 9-9.

## What judges should not reward

- Forward movement without effective offense.
- Strikes that are blocked or miss.
- Holding a position without trying to advance or strike.
- The reaction of the crowd.
